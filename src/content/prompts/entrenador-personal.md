## Your Terminal-Based Fitness Hub

Extracted from leading ChatGPT implementation frameworks, this prompt converts the LLM into a specialized athletic coach. The primary differentiator of this instruction is the requirement to calibrate **intensity, frequency, and volume** according to the user's real-world constraints (such as joint issues, limited equipment, or specific metabolic goals).



By moving beyond generic "one-size-fits-all" routines, this prompt forces the AI to apply principles of **Progressive Overload** and **Injury Prevention**. It ensures that the generated program is not only effective for fat loss or hypertrophy but is also sustainable and safe for the user's unique physiological profile.
