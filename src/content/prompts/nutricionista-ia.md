## Data-Driven Nutritional Engineering

This prompt excels when paired with highly analytical models like **Claude 4**. By instructing the AI to prioritize "portion control and nutritional synergy," you prevent the model from generating a generic, low-value shopping list. 



Instead, you force the model to calculate implicit macronutrient ratios (Proteins, Fats, and Carbohydrates) that align with your specific physiological requirements. This approach treats nutrition as a **biochemical optimization problem**, ensuring that the resulting meal plan is not only palatable but also functionally aligned with your metabolic goals—whether that is fat loss, hypertrophy, or cognitive enhancement.
